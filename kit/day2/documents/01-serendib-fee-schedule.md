# Serendib Bank: Schedule of Fees and Charges

**Effective 1 October 2026 · Document SB-FEE-2026-10**

*Serendib Bank is a fictional bank created for training. These fees are not real.*

All fees are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Savings and current accounts

| Service | Fee (LKR) |
|---|---|
| Savings account opening | Free |
| Minimum balance, Smart Savings account | 1,000 |
| Fee if below minimum balance for a full month | 150 per month |
| Current account cheque book (25 leaves) | 750 |
| Account statement, printed at a branch | 100 per page |
| e-Statement | Free |
| Dormant account reactivation | Free |

## 2. Debit cards

| Service | Fee (LKR) |
|---|---|
| Visa Classic debit card, annual fee | 500 |
| Visa Platinum debit card, annual fee | 1,500 |
| Replacement of a lost or stolen debit card | 750 |
| Replacement of a damaged card (old card returned) | Free |
| PIN re-issue | 250 |
| Cash withdrawal at another bank's ATM in Sri Lanka | 30 per withdrawal |
| Cash withdrawal at an ATM overseas | 4% of the amount (minimum LKR 500) |

## 3. Credit cards

| Service | Fee (LKR) |
|---|---|
| Serendib Gold credit card, annual fee | 3,000 (waived if annual spend exceeds LKR 600,000) |
| Late payment fee | 1,500 |
| Over-limit fee | 1,000 |
| Cash advance fee | 3% of the amount (minimum LKR 400) |
| Foreign currency transaction fee | 3.5% |

## 4. Digital banking and transfers

| Service | Fee (LKR) |
|---|---|
| Serendib Online and mobile app | Free |
| LankaPay CEFT transfer to another bank (online) | 25 |
| LankaPay CEFT transfer to another bank (at a branch) | 100 |
| Standing order setup | 200 |
| SMS alerts | 50 per month |

## 5. Other services

| Service | Fee (LKR) |
|---|---|
| Stop payment of a cheque | 500 |
| Returned cheque (insufficient funds) | 2,500 |
| Safe deposit locker, small, annual | 7,500 |

Fees are reviewed every six months. Customers are informed at least 30 days before any fee increase.
