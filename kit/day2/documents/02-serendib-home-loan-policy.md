# Serendib Bank: Home Loan Product Policy

**Version 3.2 · Effective 1 September 2026 · Document SB-HL-2026-09**

*Serendib Bank is a fictional bank created for training. These rates and rules are not real.*

## 1. Purpose

Serendib Home Loans help customers buy, build or renovate a residential property in Sri Lanka.

## 2. Interest rates

| Customer type | Rate type | Annual interest rate |
|---|---|---|
| Salaried employee (salary credited to Serendib Bank) | Fixed for first 3 years, then variable | 11.5% fixed, then AWPLR + 2.5% |
| Salaried employee (salary at another bank) | Fixed for first 3 years, then variable | 12.25% fixed, then AWPLR + 3.0% |
| Self-employed or business owner | Variable | AWPLR + 3.5% |
| Sri Lankan working abroad (NRFC account holder) | Fixed for first 5 years | 10.75% |

AWPLR is the Average Weighted Prime Lending Rate published weekly by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

## 3. Loan amount and tenure

- Minimum loan: LKR 1,000,000.
- Maximum loan: up to **80%** of the property's valuation for purchase, or 70% for construction.
- Maximum tenure: **25 years**, and the loan must be repaid before the borrower turns **65**.
- Monthly instalment must not exceed **40%** of the applicant's net monthly income.

## 4. Eligibility

- Age 21 to 55 at the time of application.
- Salaried applicants: at least 2 years of continuous employment, 6 months with the current employer.
- Self-employed applicants: at least 3 years of audited accounts or tax returns.
- A clear title deed and an approved building plan are required.

## 5. Fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Processing fee | 0.5% of the loan amount (minimum LKR 15,000, maximum LKR 75,000) |
| Valuation fee | According to the bank's panel valuer's scale |
| Early settlement fee, within the fixed-rate period | 3% of the outstanding balance |
| Early settlement fee, after the fixed-rate period | Free |

## 6. Required documents

National Identity Card (NIC); last 6 months' salary slips or audited accounts; last 6 months' bank statements; title deeds and a 30-year extract; approved building plan; valuation report.

## 7. Decision time

A decision is given within **7 working days** of receiving all documents. Disbursement follows within 5 working days of approval.
