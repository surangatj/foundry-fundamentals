# Serendib Bank: Cards, Digital Banking and Customer Security Policy

**Effective 1 July 2026 · Document SB-SEC-2026-07**

*Serendib Bank is a fictional bank created for training. This policy is not real.*

## 1. Lost or stolen cards

1. The customer must block the card immediately in the Serendib mobile app (Cards → Block card) or by calling the 24-hour hotline **011 200 0000**.
2. A blocked card cannot be unblocked; a replacement card is issued.
3. A replacement card is delivered to the customer's registered branch within **3 working days**, or by courier to the registered address within **5 working days** in Colombo and **7 working days** elsewhere.
4. The customer is not liable for transactions made **after** the card was reported lost.

## 2. Disputed transactions

- Customers may dispute a card transaction within **60 days** of the statement date.
- The bank acknowledges a dispute within 2 working days and resolves it within **45 days**.
- A temporary credit may be given for disputes above LKR 25,000 while under investigation.

## 3. Digital banking security

- Serendib Bank will **never** ask for a password, PIN, one-time password (OTP) or full card number by phone, SMS, email, WhatsApp or chat.
- An OTP is valid for **3 minutes**.
- After **3** wrong password attempts, online banking is locked for 24 hours; customers can unlock it immediately at a branch with their NIC.
- Daily online transfer limit: LKR 500,000 by default, adjustable up to LKR 2,000,000 at a branch.

## 4. Customer data and privacy

- Customer data is processed in line with Sri Lanka's **Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022**.
- Staff and digital assistants may only share account information after the customer is verified.
- Customers can request a copy of their personal data through any branch.

## 5. Branch hours

Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 15:00. Selected "Super Branches" are open Saturday 9:00 to 13:00. All branches are closed on Poya days and public holidays.
